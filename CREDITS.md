# Credits and evidence

[Back to portfolio](README.md)

MetaOrbit presents editorial case studies and original diagrams derived from a static review of privately supplied third-party n8n workflow templates. The diagrams simplify the functional relationships visible in the exports, and the examples use fictional data.

The public-ready materials contain newly written prose and original SVG artwork. Service names identify integrations shown in the concepts. No endorsement, affiliation, client engagement, or measured result is claimed.

The evidence for this portfolio is limited to template structure and configuration. Live integrations, external deliveries, compatibility, and business outcomes were outside the scope of the portfolio review.

Each editor image was captured from a separate local n8n demonstration. The captures are limited to the canvas and show standard service icons as displayed by the editor. Private data, account identifiers, real endpoint URLs, credentials, original exports, and third-party template artwork are excluded. The screenshots establish that the nodes and connections exist in the editor. The demonstrations remain inactive and unexecuted.

The lead qualification case study credits the upstream listing. Authorship, licenses, and redistribution rights for the other source templates remain unverified. The portfolio shares its own written analysis, diagrams, and editor captures of the adaptations.
